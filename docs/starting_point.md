Based on the [official website of the Physical AI Hackathon 2026](https://physical-ai-hackthon.vercel.app/), here are the core tracks along with some of the most pressing real-world problems in each domain based on current industry and environmental data:

### 🌱 1. Sustainability & Climate (Green Tech)
*Focus: AI-powered environmental monitoring, resource optimization, and sensing systems.*
* **Extreme Weather Resilience:** As climate change accelerates, predicting and mitigating the impact of extreme weather (floods, wildfires, droughts) on physical assets and public utilities is a major challenge. 
* **Soil & Water Management:** Optimizing water availability and monitoring soil health (including organic carbon content) is critical for both agriculture and preventing environmental degradation.
* **Renewable Energy Optimization:** Monitoring and maintaining renewable energy assets (like solar panels and wind turbines) at the edge to prevent underperformance and failure.
* **Complex Environmental Data:** Collecting, cleaning, and analyzing massive datasets from varied environmental sensors to make real-time conservation decisions.

### ♿ 2. Accessibility (Inclusive Tech)
*Focus: Hardware + AI solutions empowering people with disabilities.*
* **Workplace & Educational Accommodations:** There is a severe lack of physical and digital tools that adapt in real-time to varied cognitive and physical needs, preventing Persons with Disabilities (PWD) from fully participating in tech and industrial workforces.
* **Independent Navigation:** Creating reliable, edge-AI powered spatial awareness tools (using computer vision) to help visually impaired individuals navigate dynamic physical environments safely.
* **Universal Interface Adaptation:** Designing physical input/output devices (alternative input devices, real-time speech-to-text displays) that can operate offline or in low-resource environments without requiring expensive, specialized setups.

### 🏙️ 3. Smart Infrastructure (Urban AI)
*Focus: Intelligent systems for cities, buildings, and public spaces.*
* **Fragmented Legacy Systems:** The biggest challenge in smart cities is retrofitting old physical infrastructure (roads, bridges, legacy power grids) with modern IT networks and sensors without causing massive disruptions.
* **Data Capacity & Edge Processing:** The sheer volume of data generated by thousands of city sensors overwhelms standard networks. Processing data locally (Edge AI) for things like traffic optimization is critical to reduce latency and bandwidth.
* **Privacy & Cybersecurity:** As public spaces become heavily monitored by sensors and cameras, maintaining citizen privacy and securing these smart networks against cyberattacks is a massive priority.

### 🔒 4. Safety & Automation (Safety)
*Focus: Real-time detection, anomaly identification, and automated response systems.*
* **Industrial and Machinery Accidents:** Preventing severe injuries in factories and warehouses by using AI-powered machine guarding to instantly halt equipment (like conveyors or cranes) when a human enters a danger zone.
* **PPE & Compliance Enforcement:** Real-time, automated monitoring to ensure that workers are wearing the correct Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) in hazardous environments.
* **Hazardous Environment Monitoring:** Instantaneous detection of anomalies like chemical leaks, fire, or smoke in industrial settings using localized sensor data and computer vision.

### 🌾 5. Agriculture & Rural (AgriTech)
*Focus: Edge AI for crop monitoring, soil analysis, and precision agriculture.*
* **Hyper-Local Climate Vulnerability:** Smallholder farmers lack the tools to accurately predict and respond to hyper-local weather shifts, droughts, and flooding, leading to massive crop losses.
* **Resource & Infrastructure Limitations:** Rural areas often suffer from poor internet connectivity and lack of reliable electricity, meaning agricultural tech must be low-power and capable of running entirely offline (TinyML/Edge AI).
* **Precision Agriculture Affordability:** Modern automated farming (like targeted pesticide spraying or automated disease detection) is often too expensive. There is a pressing need for cheap, accessible hardware/AI combinations that can diagnose plant diseases or assess soil moisture on a budget.

### ✨ 6. Open Challenge
*Focus: Your domain, your problem.*
* *Problems in this track are entirely open-ended, provided they use AI integrated with physical hardware to solve a tangible, real-world issue.*

## Final Project Idea

- Build an edge-computing assistant robot named `DummE` using a Raspberry Pi as the on-device brain.
- The robot will process sensor and camera inputs locally for low-latency decisions, reduced cloud dependence, and better privacy.
- It can support household and lab assistance tasks such as object detection, obstacle awareness, and basic autonomous movement using the existing hardware inventory.
